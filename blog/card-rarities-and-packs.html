Card rarities and pack odds: how to open smarter

Common to Ultimate explained, the exact odds of every pack, duplicate refunds, the card exchange and the best way to spend your coins.

By HeyFootball team📅 18 October 2026⏱️ 3 min read
🃏

There are over 400 player cards to collect in HeyFootball, from everyday squad players to football legends. Knowing how rarities and packs work is the difference between a collection that grows fast and coins that disappear. Here's everything, with the real numbers.

The seven rarities

Every card has one of seven rarities. Rarer cards are generally higher-rated players, and they glow a little more in your Gallery.

RarityExchange valueDuplicate refund
⚪ Common10 points5 coins
🟢 Uncommon20 points10 coins
🔵 Rare30 points25 coins
🟣 Super Rare40 points50 coins
🟠 Epic50 points100 coins
🟡 Legendary60 points250 coins
🔴 Ultimate70 points500 coins

Every pack and its odds

Each pack gives you three players. The odds below are the chance for each of those three cards. Every pack also has its own list of about 30 to 50 players, which you can see with the "👀 See the players" button before you buy.

Which pack should you buy?

Starting out: Basic packs

When your collection is small, almost every card is new. Cheap Basic packs fill your Gallery quickly, and new cards count toward achievements like "Collect 50 players".

Building a team: Rare and Epic packs

Once you need better players for your dream team, Rare packs guarantee Rare or better on every card. Epic packs are the sweet spot for Epic players, with a small chance of an Ultimate.

Chasing the best: Legendary and Icons packs

Save these for when your collection is already big. They're expensive, but every card is Epic or better.

Tip: during Admin Abuse (Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 17:00 to 18:00 Portugal time) packs are half price. A Legendary pack costs 1,000 coins instead of 2,000, so that's the time to open the big ones.

Open several packs at once

Choose ×1, ×3, ×5 or ×10 above the Open button. Any free packs you have are used first, then coins. All the cards are revealed together, which saves time when you're working through a pile of free packs.

Turn doubles into better cards: the exchange

Doubles give coins back, but there's a better use: the card exchange in the Gallery. Trade in as many doubles as you like (you always keep one copy). The site averages their rarity points from the table above, adds 10, and gives you a random card of that rarity, so roughly one rarity higher.

  • One Common double (10) → average 10 + 10 = Uncommon
  • A Common and an Epic (10 + 50 = average 30) + 10 → Super Rare
  • Three Legendary doubles (average 60) + 10 → Ultimate

Free packs you might be missing

  • Invite a friend: you both get a free Rare pack, and every 5 friends earns you a treasure chest. See playing with friends.
  • Beat the Deadly Challenge: a treasure chest with five random prizes. Read our survival tips.
  • Gifts from the admins during special events.
