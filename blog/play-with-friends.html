Playing with friends: invites, trades, chat and challenges

Friend codes, invite rewards, card trades, group chats and challenge links: how to get the most out of HeyFootball with your friends.

By HeyFootball team📅 18 October 2026⏱️ 2 min read
🤝

HeyFootball is more fun with friends. You can trade cards, chat, compete in the tournament and challenge each other's scores. And bringing friends in earns you free packs. Here's how all of it works.

Add friends with a friend code

Every player has a 6-character friend code, like K7QX2M. Find yours in Friends → My friends and tap 📋 Copy. When your friend types it in, you're friends instantly, with no request to accept. Codes leave out letters that look alike (0 and O, 1 and I), so they're easy to read out loud.

Invite rewards: free packs for both of you

Your personal invite link is in Friends and on your Profile. When a new player joins with it:

  • they get a free Rare pack and you become friends automatically,
  • you get a free Rare pack too,
  • and every 5 friends who join earns you a treasure chest with five random prizes.

Invite rewards count for your first 20 invites. Share your link on WhatsApp with one tap.

Trade cards

Open a friend's profile to see their collection, then tap 🔄 Trade doubles. You can see which of their cards you already own, and you don't have to offer the same number of cards on each side. Trading is the best way to complete your collection. Check what your doubles are worth in our rarity guide.

Chat with friends

The Chat tab has a private chat with each friend, an "All my friends" chat, and group chats. Tap + to create one, pick two or more friends and name it. Chats are friends-only, and messages you've read on your phone also count as read on your computer.

Challenge links

After any Arcade game, tap 📤 Share my score & challenge a friend. You get a picture of your score and a link. When your friend opens it, they land straight in that game with a banner showing the score to beat. If they beat it, they're told to share it back, and the rivalry continues. The Deadly Challenge is perfect for this.

Compete in the tournament and leaderboards

You and your friends are all in the daily knockout Tournament, and you can compare totals on the leaderboards. The friendly rivalry is half the fun.

Tip: the "Squad" achievement unlocks when you have 10 friends, and "Friendly" at 3. Check them in your Profile → 🏅 Achievements.

Bring your friends

You both get a free Rare pack.
