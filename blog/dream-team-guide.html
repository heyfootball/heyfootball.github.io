Dream team power-ups: how to build a 4-3-3 that pays you every day

The new dream team rules explained: 8 power-ups, the captain who scores double, and how to build a clever team that beats one picked only by rating.

By HeyFootball team📅 18 October 2026⏱️ 4 min read
⭐

Your dream team is the most underrated feature in HeyFootball. Set it up once and it earns you points and coins every single day. Since the power-ups update it's also a proper puzzle: the highest-rated eleven is often not the best team. Here's how it all works and how to build a winner.

How dream team points work

You pick 11 players you own, one for every position of a 4-3-3. Each day, every player scores:

  • his rating, plus or minus 5 (a random number that's the same for everyone that day),
  • plus his power-up bonus,
  • and your captain scores double.

You also get coins worth half of your team's points every day, so a smarter team means more coins.

Tip: changes to your team count from the next day. The very first team you save starts earning straight away.

The 8 power-ups

Every card has one power-up, shown on the card in your Gallery, in packs and on the dream team pitch:

Power-upWhat it does
🔥 Hot streak+6 points every day
🤝 Club link+3 for each teammate from the same club (max +15)
🌍 Nation link+3 for each teammate from the same country (max +15)
👑 Leader+3 to every other player in his line (defence and goal, midfield, or attack)
🎲 Wildcardscores between −10 and +15 around his rating instead of −5 to +5
🧱 Rock+10, but only if he plays in defence or goal
🎯 Finisher+8, but only if he plays in attack
🌟 Wonderkid+10 every day (every Rising Stars player has it)

Plus the ©️ Captain: pick one player in your team and he scores double.

How to build a clever team

1. A Rock at the back beats a slightly better defender

A centre-back rated 84 with 🧱 Rock scores about 94 a day. A centre-back rated 89 without a bonus scores about 89. The lower-rated player wins every day.

2. Build links

Club link and Nation link players get +3 for every teammate who shares their club or country, up to +15. Five players from the same club, each with 🤝 Club link, add up fast. Club packs are perfect for this, since every card comes from one club. See our pack guide.

3. Stack your Leaders in one line

A 👑 Leader gives +3 to every other player in his line. Two Leaders in a back five give each other +3 and boost the other three defenders by +6 each.

4. Choose your captain carefully

The captain doubles everything, rating and bonus. The best captain is usually the player with the highest rating plus bonus, not just the highest rating. A Wonderkid or a well-linked player is often a great choice.

5. Wildcards are a gamble

A 🎲 Wildcard averages slightly more than a normal player, but some days he'll score 10 under his rating. Great if you like risk, but don't make him your only star.

6. Use the "Team ≈ …/day" numbers

When you pick a player, every option shows what your whole team would score per day with him. It counts all the links, Leaders and your captain, so it's the quickest way to spot the hidden best pick.

A sample upgrade path

  1. Week 1: fill all 11 slots with what you have, and pick a captain.
  2. Week 2: look for Rocks for defence and Finishers for attack in your collection. Use daily coins and daily rewards on packs.
  3. Week 3 and beyond: build a club or nation core with Club and Nation links, then use the card exchange to turn doubles into higher rarities.

Dream team leaderboard

Your total dream team points add up every day, and the best managers appear on the dream team leaderboard in the Gallery. The earlier you build a clever team, the bigger your total grows.
